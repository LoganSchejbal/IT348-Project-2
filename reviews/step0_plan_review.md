The plan's leakage-prevention section doesn't address scaling for Step 7 at all, only for Step 3. | Decide and document explicitly: either (a) refit a new scaler on the current training pool before each retrain round (recommended, since drift motivates this step), or (b) deliberately keep the original scaler and justify why.
